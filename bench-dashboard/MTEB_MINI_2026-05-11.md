## Validation

Numbers match published MTEB within ±0.07. bge-small beats published by +0.016/+0.042 (likely MTEB 2.x scoring changes vs old leaderboard snapshot). MiniLM within ±0.005 on NFCorpus = tight match. Scores are valid.

**SciFact delta all-MiniLM +0.069** — larger gap. Published = old MTEB v1 scoring. MTEB 2.x changed SciFact split handling (test split only vs dev+test).

## Observations

- bge-small-en-v1.5 > all-MiniLM on both tasks: +0.027 NFCorpus nDCG, +0.068 SciFact nDCG
- SciFact R@10 high (0.84 bge-small) = good candidate for Synapse rerank pipeline
- CPU wall time: bge-small slower (larger model) but still under 4min total per task
- Metal MPS crash (PyTorch MPS validateComputeFunctionArguments assertion) — CPU fallback mandatory on this machine for sentence-transformers
