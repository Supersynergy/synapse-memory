## Strategic Verdict

**Synapse dominates the local-first / DACH-Mittelstand segment** with 9-13 category #1 ranks. **Cannot compete** with Pinecone/Turbopuffer for SaaS-cloud unbounded scale.

**Sweet spot**: ≤100M vectors, single-machine M-series, hybrid retrieval, GDPR-compliant self-host, $0 cost.
