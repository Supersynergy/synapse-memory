Honest scope

synapse is a specialized accelerator, not a 100% MariaDB replacement. Stored procedures, MVCC row-locking, async replication, multi-region — not yet (P3-P5 roadmap).

What we DO have: 4 wires (MySQL+PG+gRPC+REST), 5 SuperML modules (drift, advisor, classifier, bandit, tuner), single-binary deploy, M-series Metal/MLX kernels, 1-bit Hamming compression (71×), Apache 2.0 OSS.
